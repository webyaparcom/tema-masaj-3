Kehribar evening

Signature

A long evening ritual: it starts with a foot bath, continues with a full-body warm-oil massage and ends with quiet rest.

scheduleTotal 90 minTRY 2,400

  1. Tea · 10 min — Linden tea and a short chat: pressure preference and areas to avoid.
  2. Feet · 10 min — A foot bath with warm water, sea salt and orange peel.
  3. Oil massage · 55 min — A full-body massage with long, slow strokes and warmed almond oil.
  4. Rest · 15 min — Lights low, music soft; you get up without hurry.
spa

Classic massage

First visit

A full-body massage with medium pressure and familiar strokes; for first-timers.

scheduleTotal 60 minTRY 1,500

  1. Warm-up · 10 min — A short chat and a health question (a doctor first if needed); a warm towel on the back.
  2. Massage · 45 min — Medium pressure on back, legs, arms and shoulders.
  3. Tea · 5 min — A cup of herbal tea in the rest corner.
heat

Hot stones

Evening hours

A calm massage with warm basalt stones whose temperature is checked throughout.

scheduleTotal 75 minTRY 2,100

  1. Stones · 15 min — We ask about your heat preference, test the stones on your hand and lay them on your back over a towel.
  2. Stone massage · 50 min — A slow massage with oiled, warm stones.
  3. Rest · 10 min — A few quiet minutes under a blanket.

Head, neck, shoulders

Short break

A short after-work massage, seated or lying down.

scheduleTotal 30 minTRY 850

  1. Massage · 25 min — Seated or lying down; light pressure on the scalp, nape and shoulders.
  2. Tea · 5 min — A cup of linden tea before you leave.
